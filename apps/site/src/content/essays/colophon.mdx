This is potnuse. The domain out front reads **rdmontgomery.com**; the repo underneath carries a private name. That asymmetry is the posture — a garden with the workshop door left ajar.

The frame is a rhizome, not a tree. Any page points to any page. There is no index, only a door. You came in through one; more exist.

## states

Every node wears a state. A **seedling** has just broken the ground — rough, probably wrong, shipped anyway. A **germinating** piece is actively being worked on. **Stable** means it's settled into what it wants to be. A **fossil** is kept as evidence, not as instruction.

This note is a seedling. Nothing on the site has grown past that yet — the states were a fun bet on a promotion mechanism that doesn't exist. It'll exist, someday, as its own routine.
